Most scanners need nothing else: those made since about 2015 (AirScan, Mopria) are found by
themselves on the network.

The others, and scanners plugged in by USB, need NAPS2: a free, open-source program that is not
installed by this one. Get it from https://www.naps2.com/download, before or after this
installation. Reader's Scanner says so if it finds no scanner.
